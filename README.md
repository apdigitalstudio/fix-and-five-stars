# Fix And Five Stars

Build a clean, modern, one-page website for a marketing agency that helps small contracting businesses get more Google reviews and stop missing calls. The visitor is the owner of a local appliance repair company. Tone: trustworthy, approachable, local. Not corporate. Sections in order: 1. Hero: a headline about helping appliance repair businesses get more 5-star reviews and win more jobs. A short subhead, and one button: "Get a Free Review Audit." 2. The problem: three short cards. "Not enough Google reviews," "Missed calls become missed jobs," "Competitors rank higher." 3. What you do: a simple done-for-you review system. Automatic review requests after every job, AI that responds to reviews, and the best reviews reposted to social. One line each. 4. Why it works: more recent reviews means higher Google ranking and more calls. Include a spot for one testimonial. 5. Pricing teaser: one plan, shown as "$300/month." 6. Final call to action: a contact form (name, business name, email, phone) headed "Get Your Free Review Audit." Style: blue and clean, mobile-first, big readable fonts, minimal stock photos. Every section points to one action: book the free audit. Use placeholders for agency name, logo, and phone so I can fill in my own.
